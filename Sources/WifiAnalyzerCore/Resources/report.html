Recording Details


Recordings and reports stay on this Mac. No internet addresses are probed. Missing measurements are not replaced with zeros. Chart aggregation preserves minimum and maximum values.
A router may handle ICMP replies differently from game traffic. Normal router latency does not rule out an ISP, GeForce NOW route, or video decoding issue. Correlation alone does not establish the cause of lag.
AWDL is sampled once per second; a brief activation between samples may be missed. Event time uses a monotonic clock; RTT is calculated by system ping. Original ping timestamps are preserved in ping.log.
